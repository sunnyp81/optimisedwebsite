How one fan saves the heat

A single room unit has one reversible fan and a ceramic core. For 75 seconds it pushes stale room air out, and the core soaks up its warmth. Then the fan reverses. Fresh outdoor air is pulled in through the warm core and reaches the room close to room temperature.

In summer it runs the other way round: cooler room air chills the core, and hot outdoor air gives up some of its heat on the way in. The diagram at the top of the page shows both.

Two units, one balanced room

Pair two units and they work as a team. One supplies while the other extracts, then they swap every 75 seconds, so the room stays balanced. The leader and follower link wirelessly, with no cable between them.

Wireless pairing is on selected variants. The catalogue recommends 8 to 15 m between paired units, away from metal frames and aluminium ceilings.
