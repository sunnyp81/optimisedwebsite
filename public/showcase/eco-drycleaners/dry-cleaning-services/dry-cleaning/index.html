Got a stain? Do this before you come in

What is it?
How long has it been there?
Have you tried anything on it?
  1. Blot it gently with a clean cloth. Do not rub. Rubbing can spread it.
  2. Avoid home remedies. They can make the stain harder to remove.
  3. Tell us what it is, how long it has been there, and anything you have tried.

How it works

  1. Each garment is looked over for stains and damage and tagged to you.
  2. Tell us about spills when you hand items over so they are treated before cleaning.
  3. Cleaned, then pressed and covered.
  4. Pick it up from the shop or have it brought to your door.

    Questions about dry cleaning

    How should I prepare clothes for dry cleaning?

    Empty the pockets and point out any stains. Put alterations in a separate bag with clear instructions.

    Do you collect and deliver?

    Yes. We collect and deliver across Maidenhead, Windsor, Cookham, Burnham, Beaconsfield, Marlow, Ascot, Slough. Call or send a quote request to arrange a time.

    Can you clean my dry-clean-only item?

    Usually, yes. Check the care label and ask us if you are unsure about a fabric or trim.
