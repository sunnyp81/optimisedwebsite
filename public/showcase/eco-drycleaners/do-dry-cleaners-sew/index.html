Most-Asked Questions FAQ

Do Dry Cleaners Sew Rips?

Yes, you can take ripped clothes to dry cleaners. They often have skilled tailors who’ll sew rips, mend tears, and even replace buttons. It’s a convenient way to extend your garment’s life without hassle.

Can a Dry Cleaner Sew a Button?

Yes, a dry cleaner can sew a button for you. They’re skilled in handling button replacements and repairs. Just take your garment to them, and they’ll guarantee it looks as good as new quickly.

Do Cleaners Sew on Patches?

Yes, they do. You can take your garments to a dry cleaner, and skilled staff will sew patches on them. They’ll offer various colors and styles, ensuring your clothes look stylish and well-maintained.
