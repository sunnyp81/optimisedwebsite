Alterations and repairs

Clothes you already own, made to fit you again. Hems, zips, waistbands, sleeves and tears, done so that you notice the fit, not the alteration.

Customers bring us jeans to turn up while keeping the original worn hem, suits to take in, and jumpers to shorten. Bring the item in, try it on and we will pin and mark it with you. Common jobs have set prices. Anything else is quoted before we start.

Stains other cleaners could not shift

Several of our reviews come from people whose mark had already been through another cleaner and was still there. Point out every stain when you hand things over and tell us what it was if you know. It gets treated on its own before the garment is cleaned.

Dry cleaning, pressing and laundry

Suits, dresses, coats and silks are checked over, stains are treated first, and everything is cleaned with eco-friendly products, then pressed and covered.

Shirts come back pressed on hangers, from £2.95 laundered or £4.75 dry cleaned. Everyday washing is sorted, washed, dried and folded, and duvets and pillows that will not fit the machine at home have their own prices.

Empty the pockets before you bring things in, and tell us about any spills so they can be treated before cleaning.
