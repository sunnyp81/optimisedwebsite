What Hung Gar is

Hung Gar is a southern Chinese kung fu style built on low stances, strong arms and short, heavy hand techniques. Its signature animals are the tiger and the crane.

You learn it the traditional way: stance first, then the hands, then the long forms and the weapons, such as the fan, the staff and the broadsword. Every class mixes hard conditioning with technique, so you get fitter and harder to knock down while you learn.

Classes follow the Lau family line of Lau Kar Leung, taught in Reading by Sifu Wayne Husbands, UK representative of Lau family Hung Kuen.

Root

The horse stance makes you heavy and hard to shove. Everything else is built on it.

    手法

    Thirteen hand techniques of White Tiger Fist

    Pick one to see what it does.

    Choose a technique

    01

    Ping Choy

    Level fist

    A straight punch with the fist flat, palm down, driven from the ground up through legs, hips and waist.

    02

    Bin Choy

    Side punch and back fist

    A side strike to head or body paired with a whipping back fist, powered by body rotation and wrist snap.

    03

    Pau Choy

    Upward cannon fist

    An explosive rising strike from a coiled position, the hard side of Hung Gar in one movement.

    04

    Gaw Choy

    Downward back fist

    A downward snapping arc with the back knuckles, fast and sharp, driven by sinking power from the waist.

    05

    Jin Foh Choy

    Double side arrow fist

    Both fists extend together, striking with the first two knuckles of each hand.

    06

    Kup Choy

    Downward knuckle strike

    A short downward knuckle strike for close range, useful against grabs and holds.

    07

    Sau Choy

    Inward swinging strike

    A whipping strike that swings inward to the centreline, like cracking a whip with the whole body.

    08

    Yat Chee Choy

    Sun fist punch

    A direct side punch from the horse stance with the first two knuckles. It trains power from the hips.

    09

    Yat Sing / Yee Sing Pau Choy

    One and two star cannon fist

    One star and two star cannon fists: short, explosive punches using the whole body at once.

    10

    Seong Fu Zhao

    Double tiger claws

    Both hands in tiger claw: grabbing, ripping, controlling or striking at close range.

    11

    Cam Fu Zhao

    Seizing tiger claw

    A seizing claw that grabs, controls and locks an opponent.

    12

    Wu Dip Jeong

    Butterfly palms

    Circular sweeping palms that deflect, redirect and counter.

    13

    Zhang / Zhou

    Elbow strikes

    Five elbow strikes: vertical, horizontal, downward, rear and spinning. Close range and heavy.
