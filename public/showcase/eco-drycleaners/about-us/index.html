About Eco Dry Cleaners

Eco Dry Cleaners was set up to offer good garment care without the harsher chemicals some dry cleaners rely on. We are based at 113 Bridge Road in Maidenhead and serve Berkshire households and businesses.

Our eco-friendly approach

We use eco-friendly cleaning products suited to each fabric, which helps clothes keep their shape and colour for longer. We use energy-efficient equipment where we can, and recyclable packaging.

What we stand for

  • Quality. Every garment is cleaned, pressed and packaged with the same care, whether it is a wash and fold or a leather jacket.
  • Sustainability. We choose eco-friendly cleaning products and recyclable packaging wherever we can.
  • Customer care. You deal with the people in the shop. Ask a question, make a special request, and get a straight answer.
  • Convenience. Pickup and delivery across Maidenhead and Berkshire, so cleaning fits around your week.

Local to Maidenhead

We are part of the Maidenhead community and collect and deliver in Maidenhead, Windsor, Cookham, Burnham, Beaconsfield, Marlow, Ascot, Slough.
