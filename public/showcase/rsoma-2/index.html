The story What Hung Gar is

Hung Gar is a southern Chinese kung fu style built on low stances, strong arms and short, heavy hand techniques. Its signature animals are the tiger and the crane.

You learn it the traditional way: stance first, then the hands, then the long forms and the weapons, such as the fan, the staff and the broadsword. Every class mixes hard conditioning with technique, so you get fitter and harder to knock down while you learn.

Classes follow the Lau family line of Lau Kar Leung, taught in Reading by Sifu Wayne Husbands, UK representative of Lau family Hung Kuen.

Questions

Where is Reading School of Martial Arts?

Classes run at St Bart's Church, London Road, Reading RG1 3QA.

Do I need any experience?

No. Beginners start in the Saturday all-levels class at 12pm and build from there.

What age can children start?

Children can start from age 4, in the Saturday class at 11am.

How much do classes cost?

Adults pay £50 a month for 4 sessions or £65 for 8. Children pay £40 a month for 4 sessions or £55 for 8. Ask Sifu Wayne for the tai chi rate.

Can I try a class first?

Yes. Your first class is free. Call or WhatsApp 07916 938105 to say you are coming.

Am I too old to start kung fu?

Adults of all ages train here, including people returning to martial arts in their 40s. Beginners start in the Saturday all-levels class.

Who teaches the classes?

Sifu Wayne Husbands. He has trained in martial arts since 1983 and in Lau family Hung Gar since 2001, under Mark Houghton.
