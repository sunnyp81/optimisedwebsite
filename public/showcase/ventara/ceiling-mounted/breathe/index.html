Who Breathe is for

Breathe is for buildings with a ceiling void that can take ducts. Match its rated airflow and external pressure to your ventilation design.

No ceiling void for ducts? A single room heat recovery unit uses one opening in an outside wall.

  • Total heat exchanger

    A fifth-generation total heat exchanger recovers heat and moisture from the extract air.

  • Auto bypass

    Auto bypass switches between heat exchange and bypass by outdoor temperature. It brings fresh air straight in when recovery is not wanted.

  • Ten-speed DC motor

    A plastic encapsulated DC motor with ten speeds.

  • Filtration

    A G3 primary filter cleans the supply air. An F7 medium filter is optional.

  • Options

    Breathe options include a humidity sensor, a CO₂ sensor, a smart air quality detector and a Wi-Fi module for Android and iOS control. Ask which options are in UK and Irish stock.

  • Smart controller

    Heat recovery, auto, sleep and bypass modes. Smart mode prevents frosting below -5°C and runs intermittently below -15°C. Airflow compensation adjusts for clogged filters.

  • Airtight casing

    Sealed triple folded edges stiffen the housing and keep it airtight. The casing supports parallel airflow, inverted installation and reserved side ports for outside and exhaust air.

Installing Breathe

  • Hang from four lifting lugs on suspension rods in the ceiling void. Leave the reserved access space shown on the dimension drawing: 600 mm clearance with a 450 x 450 mm opening.
  • Connect four ducts: outside air (OA), supply air (SA), return air (RA) and exhaust air (EA). Insulate the ducts marked on the installation drawing.
  • Install the unit inverted where the layout needs it. Reserved side ports take the outside and exhaust air.

Breathe questions

Which Breathe size do I need?

Pick the model whose rated airflow meets the design airflow for the building at the external pressure your duct run needs. Use the size finder on this page and check the choice with your ventilation designer.

What does auto bypass do?

Auto bypass lets Ventara Breathe bring fresh air in without passing it through the heat exchanger. The unit switches between heat exchange and bypass based on the outdoor temperature. Bypass is also one of the four listed modes.

Can it run in very cold weather?

Yes. Ventara Breathe has a low temperature routine in smart mode. It prevents frosting below -5°C and switches to intermittent operation below -15°C. Airflow compensation also adjusts for clogged filters and heat exchangers.
