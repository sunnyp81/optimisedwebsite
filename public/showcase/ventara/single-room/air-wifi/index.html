Who Air Wi-Fi is for

Homes with several units that you want to run as one system, and owners who want schedules and humidity control without walking to each room.

  • App control

    On and off, fan speed, mode, timer, weekly schedule, filter alarm, indicator lights and boost. The app is on the App Store and Google Play.

  • Group control

    Put any number of units into a group and control them together.

  • Scene control

    Create scenes from the weather or a schedule. For example, stop or switch to exhaust when outdoor humidity is above 85%.

  • Humidity target

    Set the humidity level in the app. Above it, the unit switches to exhaust until humidity falls 5% below the target.

  • Voice assistants

    Works with Alexa and Google Assistant.

  • Outdoor temperature

    The app shows the outdoor temperature and can run the unit from local weather.

Rules you can set in the app

Air Wi-Fi follows the same humidity logic as Air, and the app adds schedules and scenes on top. Here are the examples the catalogue gives.

  1. WhenIndoor humidity rises above the target you set in the app
    Air Wi-Fi doesSwitches from regeneration to exhaust
    UntilHumidity is 5% below your target
  2. WhenOutdoor humidity is above 85% (a scene you create)
    Air Wi-Fi doesStops, or runs in exhaust only, so damp air stays out
    UntilYour scene conditions are no longer met
  3. WhenYour weekly schedule or 12 hour timer says so
    Air Wi-Fi doesTurns on, off or changes speed
    UntilThe next scheduled change
  4. WhenYou tap boost
    Air Wi-Fi doesRuns at its highest speed
    Until30 minutes pass

The 75 second cycle

Air Wi-Fi has one reversible fan and a ceramic core. Watch it store heat on the way out and hand it back on the way in.

Installing Air Wi-Fi

  • Same single 158 mm hole installation as Air, for walls 280 to 470 mm deep.
  • To connect: hold the reset button for 3 seconds until the green light blinks, then add the unit in the app.
  • For paired units, the catalogue recommends 8 to 15 m apart, away from metal frames and aluminium ceilings.

Air Wi-Fi questions

Do I need Wi-Fi for the unit to work?

No. The buttons and remote still run it. Wi-Fi adds the app, schedules, groups and voice control.

How many units can one app control?

The catalogue says group control is not limited in number.

Does it work with smart speakers?

Yes, Alexa and Google Assistant.
