Single room heat recovery

Ventara Air single room heat recovery ventilator

Ventara Air is a single room heat recovery ventilator that ventilates one room through one outside-wall opening for a 158 mm duct. Every 75 seconds its fan reverses. The ceramic core stores heat from the stale air going out and hands it to the fresh air coming in.

Who Air is for

Air is for one room with an outside wall where ducts cannot run. Its duct length suits walls 280 to 470 mm deep.

Need app control and schedules? See Air Wi-Fi with app control.

  • Ceramic regenerator

    A ceramic core treated for hygiene recovers up to 97% of thermal energy with low airflow resistance. A heat storage ball regenerator is available as an option.

  • Humidity sensor

    Humidity control switches the unit from regeneration to exhaust above the set level. It returns to its previous mode once humidity is 5% below that level.

  • Auto shutter

    The shutter closes when the unit stops. It keeps out dust, insects and cold draughts.

  • Quiet DC fan

    A reversible fan runs at three speeds. Sound pressure is 18.9 dB(A) at the lowest speed when measured 1.5 m from the unit.

  • Filters

    Washable pre-filters come as standard. An F7 filter is optional.

  • Controls

    Button and remote control as standard. App control and wireless pairing depend on the variant.

Reading the light on the front panel

  • BlueRegeneration: the 75 second heat recovery cycle

  • RedExhaust only: stale air out
  • Blue flashingExhausting because humidity is high

The 75 second cycle

Air has one reversible fan and a ceramic core. Watch it store heat on the way out and hand it back on the way in.

Installing Air

  • Core-drill one hole through the outside wall for the 158 mm duct.
  • Suits walls 280 to 470 mm deep. A thin-wall kit and a 390 mm extension pipe (total pipe length up to 600 mm) are listed as accessories.
  • The accessory list includes plastic and metal outer hoods in white or black. A heated metal hood is also listed.

Air questions

How does Air recover heat with only one fan?

Ventara Air reverses its fan every 75 seconds. Warm stale air heats the ceramic core on the way out. Cold fresh air picks that heat back up on the way in. The core recovers up to 97% of the heat with no second fan and no ductwork.

Can two units work together?

Yes. The pairing variant links two units. One unit supplies while the other extracts. They swap every 75 seconds to keep the room balanced. Install paired Air units within 8 to 15 m of each other. Keep them away from metal frames and aluminium ceilings.

How often do the filters need cleaning?

Ventara Air flashes its light red after 960 hours of running to ask for a filter clean. Wash the pre-filters and refit them. Then hold Filter Reset on the remote for 5 seconds to clear the reminder.
