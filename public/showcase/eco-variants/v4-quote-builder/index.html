    Ask for a price

    Alterations you cannot see, and stains other cleaners gave up on

    The alterations bench is the part of the shop customers mention most. Jeans turned up with the original worn hem kept, suits taken in, a jumper shortened so it still looks like it came that way.

    Stains get the same patience. People bring in marks another cleaner could not shift. Tell us what the stain is and where when you hand the item over, and it is treated before cleaning.

    • Common alterations priced on the list, everything else quoted before we start
    • Alter and clean in one visit: bag the alteration items separately with a note
    • Cashmere, silk and wool handled with care
    Price an alteration

    Wedding dresses and traditional wear

    Wedding gowns can be altered before the day, then cleaned and boxed to keep afterwards. Tell us about the lace, beading and train and we will talk it through with you.

    Lehengas, sarees and heavily embroidered outfits are cleaned with attention to the trims and sequins. Describe the outfit in your quote request and we will come back with advice and a price.
