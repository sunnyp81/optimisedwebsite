Single room questions answered

Do single room heat recovery units work?

Yes. Each unit serves one room. Ventara Air, Air Wi-Fi and Air+ each use one wall opening for a 158 mm duct and reverse their fan every 75 seconds. Stale air warms a ceramic core on the way out. Fresh air picks that heat back up on the way in. Heat recovery reaches up to 97% as air first enters the core.

Single room or ducted MVHR: which do I need?

Choose a single room unit for rooms where ducts cannot run. Air, Air Wi-Fi and Air+ use a 158 mm duct through an outside wall. Choose Breathe when a ceiling void can take ducts. Breathe supplies and extracts at the same time through four duct connections in seven sizes from 150 to 1,000 m³/h.

How many single room units do I need?

The number depends on the ventilation each room needs. Ventara Air moves 30, 50 or 60 m³/h at low, medium and high speed with the F7 filter fitted. Units run alone or in pairs on the pairing variant. In a pair one unit supplies while the other extracts and they swap every 75 seconds. Send the room sizes and we will advise.

How quiet are Ventara units?

Ventara Air measures 18.9 dB(A) at its lowest speed when tested 1.5 m from the unit. It rises to 25.2 dB(A) at medium and 28.7 dB(A) at high. Air+ runs below 32.7 dB(A). Breathe ranges from 28 dB(A) for the Breathe 250 to 37 dB(A) for the Breathe 1000.

How much electricity does a single room unit use?

Ventara Air draws 4.7 W at low speed, 5.6 W at medium and 6.5 W at high. Air+ draws 6, 7 and 7.8 W. For ducted ventilation Breathe input power runs from 58 W for the Breathe 150 to 420 W for the Breathe 1000.

What wall thickness do single room units need?

Air and Air Wi-Fi suit walls 280 to 470 mm deep. Air+ suits walls 220 to 360 mm deep. All three use a 158 mm duct through an outside wall. A thin-wall kit and a 390 mm extension pipe are listed as accessories. The extension takes the total pipe length to 600 mm.
