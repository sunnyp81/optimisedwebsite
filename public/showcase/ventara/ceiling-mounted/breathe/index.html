Who Breathe is for

Whole-house and light commercial ventilation: new builds, offices, classrooms and refurbishments with a ceiling void for ducting.

  • Total heat exchanger

    A fifth-generation total heat exchanger recovers heat and moisture from the extract air.

  • Auto bypass

    Switches between heat exchange and bypass by outdoor temperature, and brings fresh air straight in when recovery is not wanted.

  • Ten-speed DC motor

    A plastic encapsulated DC motor with ten speeds.

  • Filtration

    G3 primary filter on the supply air, with an F7 medium filter as an option.

  • Smart controller

    Heat recovery, auto, sleep and bypass modes. Smart mode prevents frosting below -5°C and runs intermittently below -15°C. Airflow compensation adjusts for clogged filters.

  • Airtight casing

    Triple folded, occlusal-sealed edges stiffen the housing and seal it. Parallel airflow, inverted installation and reserved side ports for outside and exhaust air.

Installing Breathe

  • Hang from four lifting lugs on suspension rods in the ceiling void. Leave a 600 x 450 mm access space below the panel for filter and core service.
  • Four duct connections: outside air (OA), supply air (SA), return air (RA) and exhaust air (EA). Insulate the outside and exhaust ducts.
  • Can be installed inverted, with reserved side ports for outside and exhaust air where the layout needs them.

Breathe questions

Which Breathe size do I need?

Pick the model whose rated airflow meets the design airflow for the building, at the external pressure your duct run needs. Use the size finder on this page, then check with your ventilation designer.

What does auto bypass do?

When the outdoor temperature makes heat recovery unhelpful, for example on a mild summer night, the unit routes fresh air past the exchanger.

Can it run in very cold weather?

Yes. Smart mode prevents frosting below -5°C and switches to intermittent operation below -15°C.
