Single room questions answered

Do single room heat recovery units work?

Yes. Each unit serves one room. Ventara Air and Air+ units use one wall opening for a 158 mm duct and reverses its fan every 75 seconds. Stale air warms a ceramic core on the way out. Fresh air picks that heat back up on the way in. Heat recovery reaches up to 97% as air first enters the core.

What is the difference between Air and Air+?

Air+ adds a temperature sensor for free cooling, an F7 filter as standard and a curved magnetic front. Air suits deeper walls of 280 to 470 mm. Air+ suits walls 220 to 360 mm deep. Both ranges have a humidity model. Air+ also has a CO₂ model.

Single room or ducted MVHR: which do I need?

Choose a single room unit for rooms where ducts cannot run. Air and Air+ models use a 158 mm duct through an outside wall. Choose Breathe when a ceiling void can take ducts. Breathe supplies and extracts at the same time through four duct connections in seven sizes from 150 to 1,000 m³/h.

How quiet are Ventara single room units?

Air Humidity and Air Humidity WiFi measure 18.9 dB(A) at their lowest speed when tested 1.5 m from the unit. They rise to 25.2 dB(A) at medium and 28.7 dB(A) at high. The Air+ models are rated below 32.7 dB(A).

How much electricity does a single room unit use?

Air Humidity and Air Humidity WiFi draw 4.7 W at low speed, 5.6 W at medium and 6.5 W at high. The Air+ models draw 6, 7 and 7.8 W.

What wall thickness do single room units need?

The Air models suit walls 280 to 470 mm deep. The Air+ models suit walls 220 to 360 mm deep. All of them use a 158 mm duct through an outside wall. A thin-wall kit and a 390 mm extension pipe are listed as accessories for Air.
