Two ways to ventilate

Choose by the building. Rooms with no route for ducts take a single room unit. Buildings with a ceiling void for ducts take Breathe.

Single room ventilation through the wall

Each unit uses one wall opening for a 158 mm duct. The fan reverses every 75 seconds so a ceramic core can hold on to the heat.

  • Air Humidity sensor and remote control. 18.9 dB(A) at the lowest speed when measured 1.5 m away.
  • Air Wi-Fi Everything in Air, plus the app: schedules, scenes, groups and voice control.
  • Air+ CO₂ and temperature sensors with free cooling. F7 filter and IPX4 as standard. A humidity sensor is listed as an option.
