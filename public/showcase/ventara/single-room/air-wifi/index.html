Ask about Air Wi-Fi

Who Air Wi-Fi is for

Air Wi-Fi is for anyone who wants to run Air from a phone. The app handles schedules, humidity targets and groups of Wi-Fi units.

Prefer buttons and a remote only? See Ventara Air with humidity control.

  • App control

    On and off, fan speed, mode, timer, weekly schedule, filter alarm, indicator lights and boost. The app is on the App Store and Google Play.

  • Group control

    Put compatible Wi-Fi units into a group and control them together. The group has no set size limit.

  • Scene control

    Create scenes from the weather or a schedule. One example is stopping or switching to exhaust above 85% outdoor humidity.

  • Humidity target

    Set the humidity level in the app. In regeneration mode the unit switches to exhaust above that level. It returns to the previous mode when humidity falls 5% below the target.

  • Voice assistants

    Works with Alexa and Google Assistant.

  • Outdoor temperature

    The app shows the outdoor temperature and can run the unit from local weather.

Rules you can set in the app

Air Wi-Fi follows the same humidity logic as Air. The app adds schedules and scenes on top. These rules are typical examples.

  1. WhenIndoor humidity rises above the target you set in the app
    Air Wi-Fi doesSwitches from regeneration to exhaust
    UntilHumidity is 5% below your target
  2. WhenOutdoor humidity is above 85% (a scene you create)
    Air Wi-Fi doesStops or runs in exhaust only to keep damp air out
    UntilYour scene conditions are no longer met
  3. WhenYour weekly schedule or 12 hour timer says so
    Air Wi-Fi doesTurns on, off or changes speed
    UntilThe next scheduled change
  4. WhenYou tap boost
    Air Wi-Fi doesRuns at its highest speed
    Until30 minutes pass with no other operation

Installing Air Wi-Fi

  • Install Air Wi-Fi through one wall opening for its 158 mm duct. The duct length suits walls 280 to 470 mm deep.
  • With the unit powered off hold the reset button for 3 seconds until the first light blinks green. Then add the unit in the app. Four fast green blinks confirm the connection.
  • Install paired units within 8 to 15 m of each other on the pairing variant. Keep them away from metal frames and aluminium ceilings.

Air Wi-Fi questions

Do I need Wi-Fi for the unit to work?

No. Ventara Air Wi-Fi runs from its buttons and remote without a network. Wi-Fi adds the app for iOS and Android with schedules, groups, scenes and voice control. The heat recovery cycle works the same either way.

How many units can one app control?

An Air Wi-Fi app group has no set limit on the number of units. Put compatible Wi-Fi units in a group and control them together. The app also supports scenes and schedules.

Does it work with smart speakers?

Yes. Ventara Air Wi-Fi works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant once it is added to the app. Voice control is optional. The app, the remote and the buttons on the unit all still work.
