  • Anxiety 

    Feeling anxious or nervous is a normal human response to stressful situations. Anxiety can actually be helpful, as it allows us to stay alert and focused.

  • Career Counselling 

    In today’s competitive world, career counselling has become an essential stepping stone to professional success.

  • Conduct Disorders 

    Conduct Disorder is a mental health condition in children and adolescents characterized by persistent, disruptive, and antisocial behaviors that breach societal norms and infringe upon others’ rights. Children with conduct disorder may display aggression, defiance, and a disregard for rules, often leading to difficulties in school, home, and community settings.

  • Depression 

    Depression is defined as persistent feelings of sadness, loss, or anger that interfere with daily activities. While depression and grief share some similarities, depression is distinct in that it often involves self-loathing and loss of self-esteem.

  • Gender Identity Disorder 

    Gender Identity Disorder, now more commonly referred to as Gender Dysphoria, describes the discomfort or distress that some individuals feel due to a mismatch between their assigned physical sex at birth and their gender identity. This experience can deeply affect mental health, leading to feelings of anxiety, depression, and challenges in daily life.

  • Peer Pressure or Influence 

    Peer influence is when teens choose to do something they wouldn’t otherwise do because they want to feel accepted and valued by their friends. It isn’t just or always about doing something against your will.

  • Relationship Issues 

    Relationships with our loved ones, whether romantic or platonic, can bring tremendous joy, meaning, and connectedness. However, relationships can also be sources of significant stress.

  • Screen Addiction 

    Screen Addiction is an over-dependence on digital devices, similar to substance addictions such as alcohol or drugs. It refers to excessive technology use, including TV, video games, social media, and smartphone apps, that negatively impacts one’s physical health, relationships, and mental well-being.

  • Sex Education 

    Sex Education provides essential knowledge about bodily development, sex, sexuality, relationships, and decision-making skills, equipping young people to make informed choices about their sexual health. This education should be age-appropriate, culturally sensitive, and integrated into school curriculums across grade levels.
