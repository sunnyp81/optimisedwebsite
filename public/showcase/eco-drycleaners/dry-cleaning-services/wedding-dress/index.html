Wedding dress cleaning and preservation in Maidenhead

Your gown is assessed, stains are noted, and it is cleaned and boxed to keep. Tell us about the lace, beading and train, and ask for a quote.

Call 01628 631162 Get a quote

What we handle

  • Lace, silk and satin gowns
  • Beaded and embellished gowns
  • Gowns being kept as heirlooms

How it works

  1. Consultation. We talk through the fabric, designer and any areas that worry you.
  2. Spot treatment. Stains are treated individually, with attention to the hem, train and bodice.
  3. Cleaned carefully. Cleaned with eco-friendly solutions suited to bridal fabrics.
  4. Pressed and boxed. Pressed, reshaped and boxed to preserve it.

Questions about wedding dresses

How much does wedding dress cleaning cost?
Every gown is different, so it is priced on request. Send details through the quote form.
Can you alter a wedding dress too?
Yes, alterations are priced on request.
