Dry Cleaning Costs: Complete Price Guide

At Eco Dry Cleaners a laundered shirt is £2.95, a dry cleaned shirt is £4.75, a 2 piece suit is £13.50 and a double duvet is £19.95. Garment type, fabric and finish set the price, and specialist items such as wedding dresses, leather and curtains are quoted after we have seen them. The full list is on the prices page.

What decides the price of dry cleaning

The type of garment matters most. A pair of trousers is £7.50 and a jacket is £8.95, while a plain day dress starts at £13.95 and an evening or cocktail dress starts at £24.95.

Fabric and construction matter next. Silk, lace, beading and heavy embroidery take more time, which is why dresses are listed as “from” prices. Extra work such as stain treatment or re-proofing (£8.00) adds to the bill.

Alterations are priced separately. Shortening trousers is £14.00, a new zip is £14.00 and a trouser waist adjustment is £16.00.

Household items

Bulky items cost more to clean than clothes. A single duvet is £18.50, a double is £19.95, a king is £21.95 and a super king is £24.50. Feather duvets start at £30.00. Pillows start at £9.95, and feather pillows start at £17.50.

Specialist items are quoted

Wedding dresses, leather, suede, fur and curtains are priced on request, because the size, fabric and condition change the work involved. Send details through the quote form or call the shop and we will give you a price before we start.

Ways to keep the cost down

Hand over everything in one collection so you only arrange one pickup. Tell us about stains when you drop items off, so they are treated first time. Follow the care label, and do not over-clean items that are only lightly worn.

Home dry cleaning kits and hand washing

Home kits use milder products than professional cleaning, so results vary. Hand washing in cold water with a gentle detergent suits some delicate items, but check the care label first. Items marked “dry clean only” should go to a cleaner.

Most-asked questions

How much does it cost to dry clean one item?

It depends on the item. A jacket is £8.95, trousers are £7.50, and a plain day dress starts at £13.95.

How much is a wedding dress to clean?

Wedding dresses are quoted on request. Every gown differs in fabric, beading and length, so we need the details first.

Why does dry cleaning cost more than washing at home?

Professional cleaning uses specialist equipment and skilled handling, and garments are pressed and finished afterwards.
