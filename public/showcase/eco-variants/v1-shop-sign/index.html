Alterations and repairs

Bring it in, try it on and we mark it up with you. Common jobs have a set price. Anything else is quoted before we start.

  • Jeans turned up with the original worn hem kept on
  • Suit jackets and trousers taken in to fit
  • Jumpers and dresses shortened
  • New zips, waistbands and collar turn-ups
  • Prom and wedding dresses, priced on request

Stains that won't shift

Customers bring us marks that other cleaners could not get out. Point the stain out when you hand the item over and tell us what it is, so it is treated before cleaning. Cashmere and silk are handled with the same attention.

Wedding dresses and traditional wear

Wedding dresses

We alter the gown before the day, then clean, press and box it after. Tell us about the lace, beading and train and we will talk it through. Priced on request because every gown is different.

Lehengas, sarees and embroidered outfits

Heavy embroidery, sequins and beading need cleaning that protects the trim. Describe the outfit in a quote request or bring it in, and we will advise and price it first.
