About Eco Dry Cleaners

Eco Dry Cleaners is the dry cleaner and alterations shop at 113 Bridge Road in Maidenhead. People bring us suits, shirts, duvets, jeans that need taking up, wedding dresses and lehengas.

How we work

We use eco-friendly cleaning products suited to each fabric, which helps clothes keep their shape and colour. We use recyclable packaging where we can.

  • Quality. Every garment is cleaned, pressed and packaged with the same care, whether it is a wash and fold or a leather jacket.
  • Sustainability. We choose eco-friendly cleaning products and recyclable packaging wherever we can.
  • Customer care. You deal with the people in the shop. Ask a question, make a special request, and get a straight answer.
  • Convenience. Pickup and delivery across Maidenhead and Berkshire, so cleaning fits around your week.

We collect and deliver in Maidenhead, Windsor, Cookham, Burnham, Beaconsfield, Marlow, Ascot, Slough.
