Questions

Before you hand it over

What is the Eco Dry Cleaners process? +

Items are checked, stains are treated, and each piece is cleaned with eco-friendly products suited to the fabric, then pressed and returned.

Do you offer pickup and delivery? +

Yes, across Maidenhead, Burnham, Taplow, Cookham, Marlow, Beaconsfield, Windsor, Slough and Ascot. Call or use the quote form to arrange a time that suits you.

How do I prepare my clothes for dry cleaning? +

Empty all pockets and tell us about any stains. For alterations, put those items in a separate bag with clear instructions.

Can you clean and preserve a wedding dress? +

Yes. Gowns are assessed, cleaned and boxed. Wedding dress pricing is on request.
